Exploring the Cosmos with Data

Hello, I'm Sepideh Sepehri — an astrophysicist and data analyst specializing in high-redshift universe research and machine learning applications. I combine wide-field extragalactic imaging, photometric catalogs, and statistical modeling to uncover the role of galaxy mergers.

About

Bridging the gap between the cosmos and code. I utilize modern computational tools and statistical methods to process and analyze large-scale astronomical data.

I hold an MSc in Astrophysics & Cosmology from the University of Padova. My research focuses heavily on the evolution of galaxies, where my thesis involved analyzing ~500,000 galaxies from ASTRODEEP-JWST to measure merger pair fractions up to z~10. To achieve this, I developed custom Python pipelines integrating Astropy, NumPy, and SciPy to handle catalog merging and statistical uncertainties.

Currently, my focus is expanding into independent research on machine learning applications for astronomical data. I am exploring regression, classification, and clustering models using Scikit-learn to improve galaxy morphology and merger identification.

Astro Tourism

My vision for Astro Tourism merges my background in astrophysics with my hands-on experience in Rome's vibrant hospitality sector. Hosting international communities and organizing daily events at a bustling hostel taught me the power of bringing diverse cultures together—a spirit of connection I now aim to bring to the night sky.

Drawing from my comprehensive strategic proposal for revitalizing astronomical infrastructure, my goal is to transform historical sites, such as ancient caravanserais, into modern "hotel-observatories". By combining dark sky camps with immersive hospitality, we can turn science outreach into an unforgettable travel experience and drive a sustainable "Astro-Economy".
